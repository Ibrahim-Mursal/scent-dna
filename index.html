    Privacy

    Scent DNA keeps as little about you as it can, and you can delete it at any time.

    Without an account

    Your answers and photo are saved only in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent to us.

    With an account

    • We store your email address, your password (encrypted, we can't read it), your name, birthday, profile photo if you add one, and the perfumes, accords and notes you picked.
    • Your profile is private. Only you can see it when you're signed in. That includes your birthday, your favorites, accords and notes, and your "Want to try" and "I own" lists.
    • Reviews and replies you post are public. They show your name, profile photo and scent type, and anyone can open your member page to see your reviews.
    • Likes are counted, but nobody else can see which reviews you liked.
    • If you report a review, only the site owner sees the report. A review or reply reported by three different people is hidden until the site owner checks it.
    • We use your data only to show your profile and recommendations. We don't sell it or share it with advertisers.
    • If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your email address and name.

    Orders

    • When you order, we store your name, WhatsApp number, city, address and note with the order.
    • Scent DNA checks every order first. Once it's approved, the shop you ordered from gets your order details on WhatsApp so it can deliver. The shop sees only its own orders.
    • Shops never see the rest of your profile.

    Deleting your data

    Open the account menu at the top of the page and choose "Delete my account". Your account, profile, photo, reviews, replies and likes are deleted right away. To remove a single review, open it and choose Delete. Orders you placed are kept as records of the sale, but they're no longer linked to any account.

    Perfume data

    Perfume information comes from the Decant dataset of Fragrantica community data. Bottle and note photos are from Fragrantica.
